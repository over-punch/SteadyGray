steadyGray harness

Typographers speak of the “grey value” of a block of text: squint at a well-set paragraph and it should read as an even field of tone, with no line noticeably darker or lighter than its neighbours. Ragged density pulls the eye and fractures the rhythm of reading. steadyGray measures the actual ink area of every rendered line and nudges its spacing until the whole column settles into one steady, even grey.

What “working” looks like: on load, each line of the paragraph gets a slightly different letter-spacing so the overall block looks tonally even — denser lines open up, sparser lines tighten. Inspect the paragraph: its text is now wrapped in <span class="gv-line"> elements each carrying an inline letter-spacing. Resize the window and the effect re-runs (lines regroup, spacing recomputes). In the console, window.SteadyGray exposes init, refit, and destroy.
